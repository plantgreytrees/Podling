# United States Senate inquiry report, 1912

## The collision

At 11.46 p.m. ship's time, or 10.13 p.m. New York time, Sunday evening, April 14, the lookout signaled the bridge and telephoned the Officer of the watch, "Iceberg right ahead."

## Capacity of lifeboats not utilized

The vessel was provided with lifeboats, as above stated, for 1,176 persons, while but 706 were saved.
